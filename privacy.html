Privacy Policy

App: Business Wala Bhai Autopilot
Operator: The Business Wala Bhai (YouTube: @thebusinesswalabhai)
Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. What this app does

Business Wala Bhai Autopilot is a private, internal tool. It creates short videos for the YouTube channel "The Business Wala Bhai" and uploads them to that channel only. It is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to the public.

2. Google and YouTube data we access

The app requests only one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the channel owner's own YouTube channel. The app does not read, collect or store any other Google account data, viewer data, comments, contacts or personal information of any YouTube user.

3. How data is used and stored

The OAuth access credentials are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private automation environment and are used only to upload the owner's videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Video content and metadata (title, description, tags) are sent only to YouTube.

4. YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party connections. On revocation the stored credentials stop working and are deleted from the automation environment. To request deletion of any data, email us at the address below; requests are handled within 7 days.
